Our Story

A warm room,
a French table

Café Lumière began with one idea: bring the unhurried ritual of a Parisian café to a corner of Alexandria. Every morning our bakers shape butter croissants by hand, our baristas pull slow espresso, and our doors open onto a room dressed in green velvet and warm light.

Whether you're here for a quiet coffee, a pastry with a friend, or a full afternoon at a window table, we hope it feels like home — just with slightly better croissants.
